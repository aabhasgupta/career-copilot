What Career Copilot does

It discovers jobs that match your resume and preferences, scores each for fit with an LLM rubric (including H1B sponsorship signal), and helps you prepare a tailored application - which you send yourself. It then tracks every application, watches your inbox for company replies, and updates each application's status for you.

  • Discover & rank - aggregator + ATS search, ranked by fit, salary, location, recency.
  • Track - every application in one pipeline, from applied to offer.
  • Inbox sync - confirmations, rejections, and interview invites update status for you.
  • Assist, never impersonate - Career Copilot prepares; you review and click submit.
